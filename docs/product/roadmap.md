# Unimap roadmap

Only slice 0 is open. Later slices stay closed until a human launches planner for the next slice that is not open yet.

## Slice 0 — open

Scaffold `apps/api`, `apps/web`, and `apps/admin` with the locked stack, a health route, and CI for typecheck and lint. This slice does not include auth, maps, or Porto.

## Slice 1 — later

Porto ship layer and the Wayfinding pilot.

## Slice 2 — later

The remaining containers and CI boundary checks.

## Slice 3 — later

Accessible routes, schedule, and booking.

## Slice 4 — later

QR and PWA, multiple campuses, analytics, and the map editor.
